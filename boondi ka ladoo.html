From one new friend to a forever oneA Letter for You

Dear Divya,

It's funny how life works. We only met about a week ago, yet I already feel lucky to know you. Some people you talk to for years and still feel like strangers; others walk in and somehow the conversation feels like it's been going on forever. You're the second kind — and that's rare.

So here you are, turning eighteen. Eighteen is the year the world hands you the pen and says, “Go on — the rest of the story is yours to write.” And if there's one thing I've already learned about you, it's that you were born to write beautiful things, whether in your journals or in the lives of the people around you.

Here's my one wish for you, more than cake and candles: stay soft, stay hopeful, and stay relentlessly positive. Life will throw chapters at you that are hard to read — everyone gets those — but you have this quiet strength in you. Keep choosing the bright side, keep finding the good lines in every page, and watch how the whole story turns golden.

Even though I've known you for only a short while, I promise to be one of the friends cheering loudest from the sidelines. Today, the world is a little brighter because you're in it.

Happy Birthday, Divya 🤍— PIYUSH

For the reader & the writer in youLines Worth Keeping

A few lines from essays and articles — little reminders for a girl who loves words.

We read to know we are not alone.
— a line popularised in the essay/film Shadowlands, on C.S. Lewis
You can make anything by writing.
— C.S. Lewis, often quoted in essays on writing
There is no greater agony than bearing an untold story inside you.
— Maya Angelou, I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings
A reader lives a thousand lives before he dies. The man who never reads lives only one.
— George R.R. Martin, A Dance with Dragons
Keep your face always toward the sunshine — and shadows will fall behind you.
— widely attributed to Walt Whitman, in essays on optimism
Write it on your heart that every day is the best day in the year.
— Ralph Waldo Emerson, from his essay Society and Solitude
